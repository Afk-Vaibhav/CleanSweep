About CivicSweep

Cleanliness works better as a team sport

CivicSweep exists because reporting a mess and getting it cleaned used to be two completely disconnected things. We built the bridge — and made it worth volunteers' time to cross it.

Our mission

Turn civic complaints into civic action

Most people who notice a dirty street have no real way to do anything about it beyond posting a photo and hoping someone official sees it. CivicSweep gives that report somewhere to go: straight to a volunteer who can act on it, backed by a reward that makes showing up worth it.

The problem

Waste piles up faster than complaints get resolved

Overflowing bins, blocked drains and illegal dumping sites tend to sit untouched — not because nobody notices, but because there's no clear owner for the fix. Municipal channels are slow, and there's rarely a direct way for someone willing to help to actually step in and do the work.

CivicSweep closes that gap by turning every report into a claimable task with a visible reward, so the person best placed to fix it — someone nearby, willing, and motivated — can.

How the platform works

One loop, three roles

Reporters

Spot a dirty spot, submit a photo, pin and description. It's on the board in minutes.

Volunteers

Claim a task, clean the spot, mark it complete. The reward lands in their earnings.

Donors

Fund the reward pool that keeps volunteers cleaning, task by verified task.

What we stand for

Our values

Transparency

Every task, claim and reward is visible — nothing disappears into a black box.

Community

Cleaner cities happen when neighbors look out for the spaces they share.

Sustainability

Rewarded cleanups keep happening, instead of being a one-off event.

Accountability

Every claimed task gets tracked through to a verified, completed state.
