How it works

Three steps from mess to clean

Step 01

Report a Spot

Snap a photo, drop a pin, and describe the mess. Reporting takes less than a minute — no sign-up walls.

Step 02

A Volunteer Claims It

Nearby volunteers browse open tasks and claim the ones they can get to. One task, one volunteer, no overlap.

Step 03

Everyone Gets Rewarded

Volunteers earn a reward, reporters see the area cleaned, and donors see exactly where their funding went.
