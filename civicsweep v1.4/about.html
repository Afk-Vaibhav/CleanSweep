About Nagrik

Complaints alone don't fix a city

Nagrik exists because a citizen reporting a problem and a government prioritizing it used to be two completely disconnected acts. We built the pipeline between them — verification, clustering, scoring — so a report becomes evidence, not noise.

Our mission

Convert citizen feedback into infrastructure priorities

Most people who notice a broken road or a dry tap have no real way to make it count beyond a phone call that goes nowhere. Nagrik gives that report somewhere to go: through an AI pipeline that verifies it, clusters it with everyone reporting the same problem, and scores it against real ward-level evidence — population, infrastructure gaps, and growth over time.

The problem

Scattered reports don't add up to a decision

A single photo of an overflowing bin, on its own, is easy to overlook. Multiply it by hundreds of similar reports across a ward, and it's a clear infrastructure gap — but only if something is actually counting, clustering and scoring them together. Most municipal channels aren't built to do that.

Nagrik closes that gap: every report feeds a ward-level priority score, so the scale of a problem is visible the moment it crosses a threshold, not months later.

How the platform works

One report, three stages

Stage 01

Citizens Report

A photo, a location and a short description — filed in under a minute, no account required.

Stage 02

AI Verifies & Clusters

Reports are checked, scored for severity and urgency, and grouped with others describing the same problem nearby.

Stage 03

Authorities Prioritize

Wards with the highest evidence-backed priority scores rise to the top of the government dashboard.

Accountability

Built to scale

Ready for BRICS-scale interoperability
